# Probe: product hero (visual track)

Build the **first screen** of a product page as a single file at `side-N/index.html`.

Product: **Cadence** — software that schedules maintenance for a car rental fleet.
Audience: the owner of a rental company, 45 years old, opens it on both desktop and phone.

Required:
- headline, subhead, one primary action, one secondary
- a visual proof of the product (fake screenshot, diagram, mockup — drawn in HTML/SVG, no external image)
- works at 390px and at 1440px
- light and dark themes
- CSS inline in the file. Fonts from Google Fonts only. No other network calls.

Do not invent metrics, testimonials or customer logos.

Nothing else is specified, on purpose. Color, typography, rhythm, motion: the skill decides.
That is what the bench is measuring.
