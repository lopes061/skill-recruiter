# Probe: numbers panel (data track)

Build a dashboard as a single file at `side-N/index.html`.

Subject: a rental fleet. Four indicators (fleet size, utilisation, average days idle, cars in
service), one time series (utilisation by month, 12 months) and one breakdown by branch (6 branches).

Required:
- charts drawn in SVG or canvas, no charting library over the network
- readable legends and axes; every number carries its unit
- colors that survive light and dark themes and never rely on hue alone to separate series
- 390px and 1440px
- invented data, internally consistent: the branches add up to the total

Chart choice, palette and layout: the skill decides.
